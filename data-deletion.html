VisOS / Veyra — Data Deletion Request

Last updated: June 2026

You can have all of your personal data permanently deleted from VisOS at any time. There are two ways:

Option 1 — Email

Send a request from your account email to maximusblackbourne@gmail.com with the subject DELETE MY DATA. We will permanently delete your data within 30 days and confirm by reply.

Option 2 — In Facebook/Instagram

If you connected via Facebook or Instagram, you can remove VisOS in your Facebook/Instagram Settings → Apps and Websites. Meta notifies us, and we delete the associated data within 30 days.

Questions: maximusblackbourne@gmail.com
